# AI for Parkinson ICAI Lab

The [AI for Parkinson ICAI Lab](https://icai.ai/icai-labs/ai-for-parkinson) is a multidisciplinary research group focused on the development 
of AI-based remote monitoring tools for people with Parkinson’s disease. 
The AI for Parkinson Lab is embedded in the Radboudumc Center of Expertise for Parkinson & Movement Disorders, and a collaborative effort of
Radboudumc, Radboud University, and Verily.

## Open-source tools and repositories

All publicly available software, toolboxes, and research repositories are hosted in the **[Digital Biomarkers for Parkinson's Disease](https://github.com/biomarkersParkinson)** GitHub organization.
